✦ The Hook

I'm not just someone grinding for a paycheck. I'm the person who walks into the room and immediately makes you feel like you've known them forever. The first thing out of my mouth will make you laugh or grin from ear to ear — and when things get hard, I'm the one who shows up, listens, and takes action.

✦ The Journey

I'm a May 2026 Information Systems graduate from UMBC — a proud HBCU-adjacent Black woman in tech from Silver Spring, Maryland, with roots in Ghana. 🇬🇭

My path to tech wasn't linear. I led UMBC's Women in Cybersecurity chapter when it was practically inactive, rebuilt it from the ground up, organized our first campus CTF fundraiser, and watched it become a community that people actually wanted to be part of. That taught me that the most important skill in tech isn't the code — it's the people.

✦ What I Build

My technical home is at the intersection of AI security and software engineering. I've built AI-powered security monitoring systems that apply MITRE ATT&CK logic, integrated Claude via REST API for automated incident response, and deliberately tried to break my own systems to find failure modes. I think like an attacker and build like an engineer.

But I also build things just because they're cool — like PixelPlay, a browser-based AI music player that generates pixel art for every song. Because why not?
